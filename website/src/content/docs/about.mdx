Termote is an open-source, self-hosted web app that puts the terminal sessions of your machine, and the Claude Code
or Codex running in them, on your phone or another computer. The name is **Term**inal + Re**mote**.

## Maintainer

Termote is made by **Lam Ngoc Khuong**, a software engineer based in Vietnam who has spent over a decade building
systems and leading engineering teams, and who now focuses on building small, useful tools.

- Website: [khuong.dev](https://khuong.dev/en/about/)
- GitHub: [@lamngockhuong](https://github.com/lamngockhuong)
- LinkedIn: [lamngockhuong](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lamngockhuong)

## Why Termote

In the maintainer's words:

> I have used VS Code for years, but in the age of AI agents it has become too heavy for what I still do in it:
> look at files, read Markdown, and see what changed. I also wanted to reach my machine from my phone, to answer an
> agent's questions quickly instead of sitting at the computer all day. Those are the main reasons I made Termote.

## The Project

- **Open source:** the code is on GitHub at [lamngockhuong/termote](https://github.com/lamngockhuong/termote), under
  the [MIT License](https://github.com/lamngockhuong/termote/blob/main/LICENSE).
- **History:** started in March 2026; 1.0 was released on 2026-09-28.
- **Releases:** several a week. Each one is listed on
  [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/lamngockhuong/termote/releases) and in the
  [changelog](https://github.com/lamngockhuong/termote/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md), and `termote update` installs the
  newest.
- **Signed:** from 1.10.0, every release's checksums are signed with Ed25519; the installer and `termote update`
  check that signature before installing.

## Reporting a Bug or a Vulnerability

- A bug or an idea: open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/lamngockhuong/termote/issues).
- A vulnerability: report it privately, never in a public issue, as the
  [security policy](https://github.com/lamngockhuong/termote/blob/main/SECURITY.md) explains. The
  [security model](/usage/security/) describes what Termote protects and what it cannot.
